48V Power Distribution Board
Primary product image
Description

The 48V Power Distribution Board is a DIN-rail mounted, 12-channel fused distribution module designed for industrial automation panels and mobile equipment applications. Each channel is independently fused, switch-controlled, and monitored via CAN bus, giving the host controller full visibility into load current and the ability to shed or restore individual circuits under software control.

The board is designed for high-reliability environments where unmonitored power distribution is unacceptable. It replaces conventional fuse blocks with a smart, auditable alternative that integrates cleanly into existing CAN-based vehicle or panel architectures.

Specifications
Electrical
  • Input voltage 12V – 60V DC
  • Channels 12 × independently switched
  • Per-channel rating 15A continuous
  • Total board capacity 100A
  • Current sensing ±1% full scale
Communication
  • Interface CAN bus 2.0B
  • Baud rate 125k – 1M selectable
  • Node address DIP-configurable
  • Reporting rate 10 – 100 Hz
Physical
  • Mounting TS-35 DIN rail
  • Dimensions 280 × 90 × 58 mm
  • Weight 1.1 kg
  • Enclosure rating IP20
Environmental
  • Operating temp −40°C to +85°C
  • Storage temp −55°C to +105°C
  • Vibration MIL-STD-810H Method 514
  • Humidity 5 – 95% non-condensing

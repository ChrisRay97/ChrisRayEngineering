Modular Vise Stop
Primary product image
Description

The Modular Vise Stop is a precision-ground workholding accessory designed for manual milling operations. It mounts directly to the fixed jaw of a Kurt-style vise via a T-slot and provides repeatable part positioning in the X axis without indicating or edge-finding between operations.

The adjustable stop rod is hardened and ground to 0.0005" straightness tolerance. The body is machined from 1018 steel and surface ground on all reference faces. A secondary Z-axis stop arm is included for parts that require depth location as well.

Specifications
Physical
  • Body material 1018 steel, surface ground
  • Stop rod Hardened O1, ground to h6
  • Weight 0.62 lb (281 g)
  • Body dimensions 2.5 × 1.25 × 1.0 in
Performance
  • Repeatability ±0.001 in
  • Stop rod straightness 0.0005 in / 6 in
  • X travel 0 – 3.5 in
  • Z arm travel 0 – 2.0 in
Compatibility
  • Fits Kurt D688 and compatible
  • T-slot width 5/8 in standard
  • Clamp fastener 3/8-16 socket head
Included
  • Vise stop body assembly
  • Z-axis stop arm
  • 3/8-16 T-slot nut and bolt
  • 3 mm and 4 mm hex keys
